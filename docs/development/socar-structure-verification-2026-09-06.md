# SOCAR Georgia: verified structure and remaining ownership gaps

Research date: 6 September 2026. Main evidence period: 2024 reporting year.
This replaces the earlier conceptual tree as the evidence baseline for G8 modelling.
It is not a claim that all relationships remain unchanged in September 2026.

## Material corrections

1. Sakorggazi and SOCAR Georgia Gas Distribution each report **ALPHARD (GAS) HOLDINGS B.V.**
   as parent. Their participation in the SOCAR Gas operating group does not prove
   direct ownership by SOCAR Georgia Gas LLC.
2. The Petroleum branch has named subsidiary records, not unspecified placeholders.
3. SGG reports Telavgas and 19 regional gas entities. A subsidiary list is not an
   active distributor/licence list.
4. The operating **Kulevi Terminal / Sea Port** is identified by its own website as
   **SOCAR/Black Sea Terminal LLC**. The separate Reportal company-code entry
   **202352523** does not establish a second operating terminal or an SGP subsidiary.
   Keep the dated filing identity distinct from the facility name; current status is unresolved.
5. A consolidated group's subsidiary table can repeat lower-tier subsidiaries.
   Do not interpret every row in SEG's table as an immediate ownership edge.

## Gas ownership and operating-group distinction

[Sakorggazi's 2024 disclosure](https://reportal.ge/ka/Reports/OrgReportsByYear?q=208147637&year=2024)
and [Gas Distribution's 2024 disclosure](https://reportal.ge/ka/Reports/OrgReportsByYear?q=203845312&year=2024)
both identify the same Dutch parent, Alphard. Neither parent row supplies a percentage.
A [2015 statutory announcement on Matsne](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/2783648)
independently records Alphard acquiring 91.04323% of Gas Distribution's voting shares
on 9 February 2015. This historical percentage must not be reused as the 2024/2026 percentage.

[SOCAR's 2012 financial statements](https://socar.az/mediafiles/reports/maliyye-hesabatlari/en/Financial%20report%202012.pdf)
describe the acquisition of ITERA through subsidiary Alphard. This supports historical
SOCAR-group affiliation, not today's complete intermediate holding chain.

The [official SOCAR Gas history](https://www.socargas.ge/en/history) presents SGG,
Gas Distribution and Sakorggazi together as the SOCAR Gas group. It identifies Gas
Distribution as the renamed ITERA-Georgia and describes gas purchase/sale activity.
That name must not be mistaken for proof that its current role is identical to the
licensed distribution role of SGG or Sakorggazi.

## Operational assets, brands and inactive entities

SOCAR's [Georgia overview](https://www.socar.ge/en/socar-georgia) describes WayMart
as a convenience-store chain managed by Petroleum. Brand, station, depot, lab and
branch nodes must be distinguished from incorporated subsidiary companies. The
review does not establish that WayMart is a legal alias of S Markets.

SEG's [2022 filed statements and management report](https://reportal.ge/ka/Reports/GetFile/51078),
PDF pages 16 and 55, show that historical direct-investment and broader subsidiary
lists differ. The management report describes the regional gas companies, Kulevi
Oil Terminal LLC and International Railway Expedition as temporarily inactive in
2022. That status is historical; it cannot establish 2024/2026 activity or dissolution.
The same report gives Sighnaghi participation as 91.88%, whereas the 2024 portal shows
92.00%. Retain reported precision and dates; do not invent a transaction to explain rounding.

The terminal operator identifies itself as [SOCAR/Black Sea Terminal LLC](https://kulevioilterminal.com/about-us/).
Its 2024 disclosure names SOCAR as parent. SEG's separate Kulevi Oil Terminal LLC
entry has another identification code. No alias or ownership merge between them
has been proven.

## Evidence quality and access limits

- Fourteen public 2024 group-structure pages were read, retained and SHA256-fingerprinted.
  Source inventory: `.finai/artifacts/socar-structure-2024.json`; original HTML and
  manifest: `.finai/artifacts/socar-structure-sources/`.
- The reporting portal exposes group metadata publicly but requires sign-in for the
  current financial-report attachments. No login gate was bypassed. The independently
  accessible 2022 PDF was inspected as historical evidence.
- Sakorggazi's public parent table was additionally verified in the browser.
- S Markets's parent row correctly identifies Petroleum by code 202352514 but labels
  the parent's country Azerbaijan. Preserve this source inconsistency rather than
  changing the legal entity's country automatically.
- Blank parent percentages remain unknown. SEG's parent identifier `0000001` must not
  be accepted as a verified company registry identifier for SOCAR.
- Empty subsidiary tables are not proof that a company has no subsidiaries.
- No current certified register extracts, shareholder registers or Alphard's current
  Dutch ownership filings were obtained. The research establishes a stronger dated
  baseline, not an exhaustive certified September 2026 ownership chart.

## G8 implementation consequences

Keep legal ownership, group/management membership, consolidation participation,
operating responsibility, licences and brands as separate typed relationships.
SGG business navigation can include Sakorggazi while legal ownership points to the
evidenced parent. Preserve the existing source-bound Sakorggazi identity; propose a
reviewed identifier binding before connecting it to registry code 208147637.

Do not duplicate all SEG-listed subsidiaries as direct children when lower-tier
disclosures identify an intermediate parent. Do not activate gas licences merely
because a legal entity name contains Gas. Partial stakes require separate ownership
and consolidation policies; they are not blanket instructions to multiply every
financial line by the ownership percentage.

The previous conversation tree and local structural note remain historical context.
This report supersedes their unsupported immediate-parent and generic terminal claims.
No canonical ownership mutations or financial consolidation were executed by this research.

## SGP / Kulevi recheck following user challenge (6 September 2026)

The previous wording was too categorical for an operating-company chart.
“Kulevi Oil Terminal” commonly names the facility operated by Black Sea Terminal,
not a second proven operating terminal. The operator explicitly identifies itself
as SOCAR/Black Sea Terminal LLC: https://kulevioilterminal.com/ .

Fresh reads of the public 2024 Reportal tables reconfirmed:
- Black Sea Terminal [204892170] names SOCAR as parent.
- SGP [202352514] names SEG as parent and lists S Markets, Obiekti, Industri
  Company and Petroleum-Samtredia as subsidiaries. Neither terminal entry appears
  in that SGP subsidiary table. This does not prove absence of every other relationship.
- The separate code 202352523 has an empty own group table. Its SEG listing alone
  does not prove current activity, physical asset ownership, or direct parentage.

SGP's actual operating infrastructure must be researched independently. SOCAR's
28 October 2024 article identifies SGP's Kiziki oil base, depot and laboratory,
including fuel receipt, quality control and onward transportation:
https://www.socar.ge/en/news/employee-visit-to-socar-oil-base .
Its current retail website presents fuel stations and Way Mart:
https://sgp.ge/ . These are facility/operating/brand relationships, not additional
incorporated subsidiaries by default. No evidence obtained establishes Black Sea
Terminal as an SGP subsidiary. No ownership edge was changed in the application.
