## Official evidence

- https://www.socargas.ge/en/history lists SOCAR Georgia Gas LLC, SOCAR Georgia Gas
  Distribution JSC and Sakorggazi JSC together as the SOCAR Gas group. It explicitly
  identifies SOCAR Georgia Gas LLC as a subsidiary of SOCAR Energy Georgia.
  Sakorggazi is described as a distinct joint-stock company conducting gas supply,
  distribution and network construction/operation. Ten distribution license holders
  were merged into Sakorggazi in 2013; this historical statement does not justify
  recreating ten current subsidiary companies.
- https://www.socar.ge/en/management/alexander-khetaguri describes shared management
  responsibilities across the three companies; management overlap alone does not
  establish direct share ownership.
- https://socargas.ge/ge/news/details/36 describes modifications to the distribution
  licences of SOCAR Georgia Gas and Sakorggazi in 2023. This supports distinct
  operator/licence identities; it is not a complete current licence register.

The pages reviewed establish group affiliation, activities and shared management.
They do not state Sakorggazi's immediate shareholder, ownership percentage or the
effective dates of that shareholding. The user's direct-parent statement is retained
as user-provided business context, not presented as independently verified ownership.

## Implementation consequence

Present Sakorggazi under the SGG business/group scope and count its implementation
as SGG progress. Preserve its existing canonical LegalEntity and source account,
journal, procurement and analytical bindings. Connect the group hierarchy through
typed relationships; do not turn Sakorggazi into an alias of the SGG legal entity.

Keep operational group membership, direct legal ownership and financial consolidation
membership distinct, with evidence and effective dates for each. The existing
Sakorggazi source IDs must be reused in that hierarchy. Parent/group reporting must
apply explicit consolidation and intercompany rules rather than simply summing
parent and subsidiary source totals. Licences, service areas, tariffs and obligations
must resolve to the applicable legal operator and period.

This research note corrects project interpretation. It does not claim a new canonical
corporate relationship has been published, or that SGG delivery is complete.
